About Me

My name is Nan Aye Mizu Nwe.I am a high school student studying at the Taunggalay Monastic Education School. My special interest is hydroponics technology. I find this technology fascinating because it allows plants to grow without soil, using only water and nutrient solutions.

Currently, I am working on a science project related to hydroponics. I observe daily plant growth, take photographs, and maintain detailed records. By combining my interest in photography with this project, I systematically document each developmental stage of the plants.

On this website, I’ll be sharing my discoveries, the fundamental principles of hydroponic technology, practical techniques, and my personal experiences. You’ll also get to see some of my beautiful photos along the way. Soilless cultivation (hydroponics) is the future of agricultural technology—an efficient and eco-friendly method that optimizes water and nutrient use. Join me as we explore sustainable farming together.
